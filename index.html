PROJECT 01 // AEROSPACE PROPULSION

Cable Driven Thrust Vector Control (CDTVC) System

Aerospace Engineering Mechanical Design Thrust Vector Control

The Cable Driven Thrust Vector Control (CDTVC) System is an aerospace engineering project focused on developing an alternative mechanism for controlling rocket thrust direction using a cable-driven arrangement.

The system utilizes high-strength steel cables, precision pulleys, and electromechanical actuators to achieve controlled movement of the thrust vector. The complete mechanical assembly was designed and modelled in SolidWorks, with a 3D animation created using Blender to demonstrate the mechanism and its working principle.

My Contributions:

  • Designed and developed the 3D mechanical components and assembly using SolidWorks.
  • Modelled the cable routing, pulley arrangement, and actuator mounting system.
  • Created a 3D animated demonstration of the mechanism using Blender.
  • Contributed to the mechanical design and visualization of the thrust vector control concept.
